For My Beautiful Sumu ❤️

Every flower blooms beautifully... But none of them can match your smile.

A Letter To Sumu 🌸

Dear Sumu, Every Lily carries elegance. Every Rajanigandha carries a beautiful fragrance. Yet whenever I think of you, both become ordinary. Because your smile, your kindness, your innocence, and your heart make you far more beautiful than any flower in this world.

When the wind touches the flowers, I remember your gentle words. When the stars shine, I remember your eyes. When the moon lights up the night, I remember your beautiful face.

If every Lily represented beauty, then you are the garden. If every Rajanigandha represented fragrance, then you are the spring. You are not just a flower... You are the reason every flower looks beautiful.
